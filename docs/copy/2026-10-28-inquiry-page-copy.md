# Inquiry page copy (2026-10-28)

Customer-visible wording. Draft, objection, final. The 2026-10-24 archive in
`inquiry-emails.md` still holds the form strings that did not change. The
lines below supersede the sentences that claimed a pattern or a file was
attached on a page that has neither.

## Lede

Old: "One business day, one reply. Tell us what the project needs; the patterns you attach travel with the message."

Draft: "One business day, one reply. Write what the project needs."

Objection: "Write" can sound like a specification document. "Tell us" was the honest half of the old sentence. Neither may mention attached patterns: this page's form has `pattern: null`, no pattern row, and no file input.

Final: "One business day, one reply. Write what the project needs."

"Write" stays. It is the instruction a visitor can actually follow in the message field. The false clause is gone.

## Page note under the form

Old: "Prefer chat? The WhatsApp button in the form opens a message pre-filled with your details and your pattern list — nothing is submitted through this page."

Draft: "Prefer chat? The WhatsApp button in the form opens a message pre-filled with your details. Nothing is submitted through this page."

Objection: "your pattern list" names a mechanism the site does not have. "your details" matches what the button actually prefills (name, company, message) and matches the form's own note.

Final: the draft.

## What happens next, step 1

Old: "We read the inquiry and the attached patterns."

Draft: "We read the project details in your inquiry."

Objection: "attached patterns" is a promise the page cannot keep. "what you send" was the first cut and was too vague — it could be read as a file upload.

Final: "We read the project details in your inquiry."

## What to include

Old: "Drawings or site photos, if you have them"

Draft: "Drawings or site photos, sent in reply to the confirmation email"

Objection: the list sat under "What to include for faster quoting" and read as if the form took the file. The form hint already says drawings travel by reply. The list item has to say the same thing.

Final: the draft.

## FAQ, first answer

Old: "Drawings, elevations, or clear site photos with the opening dimensions. A material and finish target helps, even if it is only a reference image."

Draft: "Opening dimensions, plus drawings or site photos if you have them. This form does not take a file — reply to the confirmation email and it reaches the same person."

Objection: the question is "What files should I send first?" An answer that only lists files, with no channel, implies the form accepts them. It does not.

Final: the draft.

## Form note when no pattern is attached

Old, unconditional: "The WhatsApp button opens a chat pre-filled with your details and this pattern. Choosing WhatsApp does not submit this form."

Kept when a pattern is attached. That sentence is true there, and the 2026-10-24 review already passed the "does not submit" clause.

Draft, no pattern: "The WhatsApp button opens a chat pre-filled with your details. Choosing WhatsApp does not submit this form."

Objection: dropping only "and this pattern" is the whole change. Do not invent a substitute object.

Final: the draft. The pattern sentence stays on a form that has a pattern.

## Quote dialog

Old: "Send your drawing or tell us the pattern, size and finish. We reply with quote guidance within one business day."

Draft: "Tell us the pattern, size and finish. We reply with quote guidance within one business day."

Objection: "Send your drawing" is the same false upload. The dialog can carry a pattern from the current page; asking the visitor to name pattern, size and finish is something the form actually collects.

Final: the draft.

## Confirmation checkbox

Restored, not rewritten: "I confirm this inquiry is for an architectural or commercial project."

Error, if it is empty: "Please confirm this inquiry is for an architectural or commercial project."
