Why PVD outlasts paint:

  • Bonding — molecular-level deposition, not a surface layer
  • Base — 304/316 stainless steel, inherently corrosion-resistant
  • Indoors — no UV degradation, no oxidation, no color shift

Custom color-matching available. Send a Pantone reference — we'll produce a test chip.

AFP (Anti-Fingerprint) nano coating:

  • Blocks oil and moisture adhesion on all finishes
  • Adds minimal cost, cuts cleaning frequency 60–70%

Daily maintenance:

  1. Soft damp microfiber cloth — removes 95% of marks
  2. Stubborn spots: mild SS cleaner, no bleach, no abrasives
  3. Wipe with the grain, not in circles

"Hairline or satin for any space with daily traffic. Mirror looks stunning on day one — clients notice fingerprints by day three." — Project Engineering
