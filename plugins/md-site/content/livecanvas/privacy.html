1. Who we are and what this page covers

Metal Divider (info@metal-divider.com) operates metal-divider.com, an inquiry-based B2B site for architectural metal screen patterns. We offer no accounts, no checkout, and no payment. The only personal data we process is what the inquiry chain needs: the inquiry form, the messages you send us, and the technical records a hosted website keeps.

2. What we collect

  • Inquiry form — name and email; country, project scope, inquiry route, and estimated quantity, each chosen from the form's list; company and phone, both optional; and a message, required only when no pattern is attached. Sending the form also requires a confirmation that the inquiry is for an architectural or commercial project. That confirmation is not stored.
  • Pattern — one pattern at most, taken from the page where the form is opened. The record stores that pattern's name, code, and slug, and a link to the pattern.
  • Inquiry record — a successful submission also stores the UTC submission time, the page, module, and button the form came from, the IP address, a User-Agent summary of at most 300 characters, a language marker fixed to English, and a Turnstile status of verified or not configured. The Turnstile token is not stored. A failed check does not create an inquiry record. A ten-minute rate-limit counter stores timestamps under a hash of the IP address, not the address itself.
  • Mail copies — the same successful inquiry is copied into two queued emails: an internal notification with the form fields, the pattern, the source, and the verification status, and a confirmation sent to the email address entered. Those copies do not include the IP address or the User-Agent.
  • Server logs — Hostinger keeps ordinary request logs (IP address, time, and the page requested) for operating the site.
  • WhatsApp — choosing WhatsApp does not submit the form and does not create an inquiry record. It opens a chat to +852 6473 1687. The prefilled message can include a short greeting, the pattern name and code, and a source reference. After the name, company, or message is typed, the form's WhatsApp link can also include those three. Email, phone, country, project scope, inquiry route, and quantity are not included.

3. Why we process it, and the legal basis

We process inquiry data to answer your inquiry and prepare quotes — steps preceding a possible contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Server logs and the inquiry form's spam check — Turnstile verification and the rate-limit counter — run on our legitimate interest in operating a secure site (Art. 6(1)(f)). Equivalent bases apply in the other jurisdictions listed in section 8.

4. Cookies

Public pages do not set a session cookie, and the site does not use local storage. The only cookie is set on the login page:

The inquiry form's spam check uses Cloudflare Turnstile. Turnstile's default is a one-time token. When the check runs, that token and, if known, the IP address are sent to Cloudflare's siteverify endpoint. The token is not stored.

There are no analytics or advertising cookies on this site.

5. Sharing and processors

We do not sell data and run no tracking pixels. Hostinger hosts the site and serves it through its CDN. Cloudflare is not that CDN: when the inquiry form's spam check runs, Cloudflare receives only a one-time Turnstile token and, if known, the IP address. Inquiry mail is sent by wp_mail on the server. No other processor is used for the inquiry form.

6. Retention

Inquiry records are kept for the tracking cycle of a B2B project, then deleted or anonymized. Server logs rotate on the host's standard schedule.

7. Cross-border transfers

This site is hosted in a data center in Frankfurt, Germany, inside the EEA. Where a processor we rely on operates outside the EEA, transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, supplemented where processors hold equivalent certifications.

9. Children, security, changes, contact

The site addresses professional adults; it is not directed at children. Data moves over TLS and is stored on processors' hardened infrastructure; access to inquiry records is limited to the team answering them. When this policy changes, the change appears on this page.
