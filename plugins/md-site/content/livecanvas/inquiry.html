Response flow

From Inquiry to Quote Route — 5 Steps

Each step has a gate. You know who reviews the brief, what gets confirmed, and what comes back next.

  1. Inquiry Intake

    Inquiry Intake + Scope Triage

    • We read the project type, dimensions, finish intent, and delivery target from your brief
    • Missing inputs are flagged before the request moves deeper

    What We Check

    Project type, size, finish, delivery target

    Response Window

    Within 24 hours

  2. Technical Review

    Technical Review + Feasibility Check

    • Our team checks grade, thickness, open area, mounting assumptions, and exposure condition against the files you shared
    • Risks are identified before price direction is confirmed

    What We Check

    Grade, thickness, mounting, exposure

    Response Window

    Within 24h

  3. Clarification

    Clarification Request if Inputs Are Missing

    • If drawings, finish references, or quantity bands are incomplete, we reply with the exact missing items
    • This keeps the quote cycle tight instead of sending a vague estimate

    What We Check

    Missing files, finish refs, quantity bands

    Response Window

    Next reply

  4. Quote Route

    Quote Route + Lead-Time Direction

    • Once the brief is aligned, we return the pricing route, lead-time band, and sample or production path that fits the project stage
    • The buyer sees what can move now and what needs approval first

    What We Check

    Price route, lead-time band, project stage

    Response Window

    After alignment

  5. Next Files

    Next Files for Sample or Production Booking

    • We close the loop with the exact files or sign-offs needed for sample release, finish approval, or production booking
    • You know what the next handoff is before the thread goes quiet

    What We Check

    Sample release, finish approval, booking docs

    Response Window

    Same thread

Contact FAQ

Questions before you submit a project brief.

Use these answers to prepare your files and shorten the quote cycle.

What files should I send first?

Share plan or elevation files, target dimensions, finish direction, and delivery timeline in your first message.

Can I ask for grade and thickness advice if details are not final?

Yes. Share exposure condition and intended use so we can recommend a draft route before your final drawing package is complete.

Do you support phased shipments for large projects?

Yes. Tell us package sequence and site handover windows, then we can align production and packing by phase.

How fast can I get a first response?

Most inquiry routes receive a first technical reply within 24 hours once basic project scope is provided.
