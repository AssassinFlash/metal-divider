Response flow

From inquiry to quote route — 5 steps

Each step has a gate. You know who reviews the brief, what gets confirmed, and what comes back next.

  1. Inquiry intake + scope triage

    We read the project type, dimensions, finish intent, and delivery target from your brief.

    Missing inputs are flagged before the request moves deeper. Response window: same day.

  2. Technical review + feasibility check

    The screen family, panel size, and fixing approach are checked against what we can build.

  3. Clarification request if inputs are missing

    If a dimension or finish is unclear we come back with a specific question, not a generic chase.

  4. Quote route + lead-time direction

    You get the material and finish direction with the delivery path, before any drawing is committed.

  5. Next files for sample or production booking

    Approved drawings, sample request, or production slot — whichever the project is ready for.

Contact FAQ

Questions before you submit a project brief.

Use these answers to prepare your files and shorten the quote cycle.

What files should I send first?

Drawings, elevations, or clear site photos with the opening dimensions. A material and finish target helps, even if it is only a reference image.

Can I ask for grade and thickness advice if details are not final?

Yes. Send the application and the exposure conditions; we come back with the grade and thickness that fit, and say what would change the answer.

Do you support phased shipments for large projects?

Yes, when the project breaks into zones or phases. Say which zone is needed first and the site schedule; the quote route follows that.

How fast can I get a first response?

Within 24 hours, and often the same day. A brief with dimensions and a finish target gets a substantive reply instead of a question list.
