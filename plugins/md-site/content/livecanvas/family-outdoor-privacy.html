Why PVD outlasts outdoor paint and powder coat:

  • Molecular bonding — PVD is vapor-deposited at the atomic level into the steel surface, not a layer sitting on top. It doesn't peel, chip, or delaminate.
  • UV-stable pigments — mineral-based PVD color resists photo-bleach. Outdoor paint fades in 3–5 years; PVD holds 10–15.
  • 304 / 316L base metal — the substrate itself resists corrosion. Even with a surface scratch, a nick reveals more stainless, not steel that rusts.

Bronze-toned stainless delivers the warm patina architects want for stone and water-adjacent landscapes — without the rust runoff that stains marble, travertine, and pool coping.

FinishBase MaterialRunoff Risk
Bronze PVD304 Stainless SteelNone — inert surface, zero leaching
Gold or Black PVD304 Stainless SteelNone — no interaction with stone
316L Bronze PVDMarine-grade 316LNone — recommended for direct pool or fountain contact

Why this matters for landscape-adjacent installs:

Architects ask for the weathered-metal aesthetic without the runoff problem. Weathering steel patinas to a warm rust-brown that pairs beautifully with stone — until the first heavy rain washes iron oxide onto Italian marble, travertine coping, and poured concrete. The stain is permanent without chemical treatment.

Bronze PVD on 304 stainless delivers the same warm patina with zero runoff risk. The PVD layer is a vapor-deposited titanium-bronze ceramic surface — inert, with no free iron to oxidize. The aesthetic stays; the runoff goes to zero.

Proven on the Shanghai Heritage Estate — 950 sqm Bronze PVD screens installed fountain-side against Italian marble. Two years in: zero staining, zero color shift on either surface. Pool-adjacent installs upgrade to 316L for chlorine chemistry.

Share your stone type, water proximity, and target finish direction — your engineer matches the exact PVD color to your palette and confirms the right grade for zero runoff.

The sample flow from spec to sign-off:

  1. You specify — which variant (Mountain / Contour / Courtyard), thickness, PVD color, and pattern direction. A three-sentence message is enough to start.
  2. We fabricate — one full-spec sample panel. Same 304 stainless, same PVD chamber, same laser cut or stamping as your production run. A real production-spec piece, not a swatch board.
  3. We ship — 5–10 days from specification lock. Air freight to your office or jobsite, DDP available for US, EU, Australia, and Gulf destinations.
  4. You hold it — check color under your site's morning, afternoon, and evening light. Photograph it against your stone palette. Show it to the client. Hand it to the foreman.
  5. Approve, order, offset cost — sample fee applies when you order the panel; the full amount credits to your production PO on order.

What the sample proves before you cut the PO:

  • Real color under real light — PVD renders differently in direct sun vs. shade. The sample tells you the truth before production locks.
  • Pattern scale and visual weight — a digital render lies about how bold or quiet a pattern feels at human scale.
  • Edge quality and gauge feel — your foreman wants to hold the weight and inspect the laser edge before install day.

Reply with your variant, PVD color, and shipping address — your sample spec sheet and cost quote return the same business day.

When to bring a structural engineer into the project:

  • Panels over 2.4m tall — wind load requires sealed anchoring drawings
  • Coastal sites within 500m of shoreline — wind-zone calc + 316L upgrade + reinforced footings
  • Alpine installs with snow load — bracket-to-structure calc for freeze-thaw cycling
  • Pool or fountain adjacency — engineer sign-off for liability and water-code compliance

Wind-load spec sheets, anchor-pull calculations, and architect-sealed drawings are available on request for any panel size and climate zone — included at no cost for Tier 2 (architect-collaborated) and Tier 3 (fully bespoke) projects.
