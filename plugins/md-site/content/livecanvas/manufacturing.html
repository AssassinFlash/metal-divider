MANUFACTURING METHODS

Laser Cutting

Fiber-laser cutting at ±0.1mm tolerance. Complex geometries, custom patterns — any motif, any scale. 3,000×6,000mm max sheet.

±0.1mm precisionSS 304 / 316Up to 3,000×6,000mm
View Laser Cut Panels

Perforation

CNC-punched uniform patterns. Round, square, slot, or custom punch geometry. High repeatability across large quantities.

High repeatabilitySS 304 / 316Up to 2,000×4,000mm
View Perforated Panels

Expanded Metal

Slit-and-stretch process — no material waste. Diamond, hexagonal, micro, and raised-flat mesh types. Structural-grade panels for heavy-traffic screening.

Zero material wasteSS 304 / 316Up to 3,000mm width

How We Work

From Drawing to Delivery — 5 Steps

Each step has a gate. Nothing moves forward without sign-off.

Drawing Review + Route Match

  • Your drawing is checked against laser cutting, perforation, and expanded metal routes
  • Tolerance, sheet size, pattern density, and finish path are confirmed before quoting

What We Check

Route, tolerance, sheet size, finish path

Lead Time

24h

Sample or First Article Check

  • A sample or first article is reviewed before the batch starts
  • Burr level, flatness, hole quality, and visual finish are checked against the approved direction

What We Check

Burr level, flatness, finish, pattern quality

Lead Time

3-5 days

Cutting, Punching, or Expanding

  • The matched production route is used for the full batch
  • Laser, punching, or slit-and-stretch production stays tied to the approved drawing and tolerance notes

What We Check

Laser, perforation, expanded metal route

Lead Time

Per batch

Deburr + Surface Finish

  • Edges are cleaned and the surface finish is applied
  • Brushed, mirror, powder coat, or PVD finish is matched to your approved sample or spec note

What We Check

Deburr, polish, brushed, mirror, PVD

Lead Time

1-3 days

QC Photos + Export Packing

  • Panels are photographed, protected, labeled, and packed for shipment
  • You receive the final photo record before the order leaves the factory

What We Check

Photo report, labels, protective film, crate

Lead Time

Same day
