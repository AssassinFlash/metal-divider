Cost drivers:

  • Pattern density — fine detail and high open-area add 15–30% to laser time
  • Thickness — 2.0mm adds ~15%, 2.5mm adds ~25% over 1.5mm baseline
  • PVD finish — standard Bronze, Champagne, Black at baseline; custom Pantone match adds $30–$50/sqm
  • 316L upgrade — coastal, spa, or humid F&B adds 20–28% over 304

Send panel count, dimensions, and pattern direction. Line-item quote returns within 24 hours.

Density drives both cost and visual impact. More cut lines, micro-bridges, and high open area mean more laser time and deburring. The cutout size controls how light and air pass through.

DensityOpen AreaBest ForTrade-off
Low15–25%Backdrops, foyers, subtle privacyStable, calm
Medium25–40%Restaurant dividers, lobby insertsBest balance for most hospitality
High40–55%Backlit walls, decorative statementsDramatic; needs thicker gauge or frame
Very High55%+Feature art, low-touch areasMaximum impact; needs structural care

For 5-star restaurant or villa entryways: 25–40% open area with 8–15mm bridge widths. Delivers privacy, shadow play, and legibility while staying structurally sound.

Send artwork and viewing distance. Density review and cost markup within 48 hours.

Mirror bronze or high-polish finishes need a lighting mockup to check for downlight hot spots. Brushed and hairline finishes diffuse light predictably.

Send reflected ceiling plan and lighting spec. Cavity depth, fixing strategy, and finish recommendation within 5 working days.

Stainless steel shapes privacy and sightlines; it does not absorb sound. Acoustic comfort comes from upholstery, fabric backing, and ceiling treatment.

For restaurant booths: pair a medium-density lattice (25–40% open area) with upholstered banquettes and an acoustic ceiling. For sound separation, we engineer a dense lower zone to seated eye level with fabric or timber backing, open pattern above for light.

Send room volume and acoustic priority. Layered partition strategy within 48 hours.
