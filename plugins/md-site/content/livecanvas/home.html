Project FAQs

What drawings or files help you quote accurately?

Elevations or a dimensioned sketch with opening width, height, and fixing zone. A finish reference and the quantity band shorten the first reply further.

How should I choose material, thickness, and finish?

Send the application and the exposure conditions. Interior partitions usually sit at 1.5–3mm; exterior and structural runs move up, and coastal sites move to 316L.

How do you plan export packing and shipping?

Reinforced wooden crates with protective layering, photographed before dispatch. We quote EXW, FOB, CIF, or DDP depending on what the project needs.
