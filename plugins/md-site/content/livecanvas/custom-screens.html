Project FAQs

What drawings or files help you quote accurately?

DWG, DXF, PDF, or a site photo with rough width and height is enough to start. We review panel split, fixing method, material thickness, finish feasibility, and whether the design needs a frame, hidden backing, or installation sequence labels.

  • Best files: DWG or DXF with dimensions.
  • Useful backup: marked-up PDF, elevation drawing, or site photo.
  • For early concepts: send the opening size, target finish, and application area.

Before quoting, we flag the project variables that change cost: panel size, quantity, thickness, pattern density, finish, and packing requirement.

How should I choose material, thickness, and finish?

Start with the application, then select the stainless grade and finish. Interior dividers, outdoor privacy screens, facade inserts, and high-touch commercial areas all need different checks.

Project conditionTypical direction
Interior divider or lobby screenSS304 with brushed, mirror, antique, or PVD finish.
Outdoor screen or coastal projectSS316 or SS316L, with finish and fixing reviewed together.
Facade or large panelsThickness, stiffening, subframe, and wind exposure need review before pricing.

If color approval matters, request small finish samples first. If transparency or edge quality matters, a larger mock-up panel is safer than a small swatch.

How do you plan export packing and shipping?

Packing is planned before dispatch, not after production. For export orders, we review protective film, foam separation, plywood crate structure, gross weight, and install-sequence labels when needed.

  1. Confirm destination country and delivery method.
  2. Separate panels by finish surface and edge risk.
  3. Use reinforced plywood crates with internal protection.
  4. Label crates so site teams can identify panel order during installation.

For fragile finishes, large panels, or mixed-size orders, we can discuss crate layout before final packing.
