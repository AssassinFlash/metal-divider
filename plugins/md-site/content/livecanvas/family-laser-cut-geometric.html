Three pattern families, each quoted per project — the quote follows pattern complexity, material thickness, and surface finish.

Pattern TypeWhat's IncludedBest For
Linear Grid304 SS + brushed/mirror finish + straight-line geometryOffice partitions, hotel transitions

New Chinese Lattice304 SS + PVD bronze/rose gold + heritage lattice geometryPrivate dining, showroom entries, residential foyers

What shapes the quote:

  • Pattern complexity — a linear grid cuts fastest; intricate carving and fine lattice add cutting and finishing time
  • Material thickness — 0.8–1.2mm is the standard range; 2–3mm carries more material and slower cutting
  • PVD finish — brushed and mirror are the simplest; antique bronze or a custom colour match adds a finish-development step
  • Quantity — repeated panels spread the setup cost across the run

Why PVD outlasts paint:

  • Bonding — molecular-level deposition, not a surface layer
  • Lifespan — 10–15 years indoors with zero color shift
  • AFP option — anti-fingerprint nano coating reduces smudges by 80% in high-touch areas
